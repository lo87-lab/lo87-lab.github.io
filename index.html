04 / Selected Work

Engineering Projects

High School Senior Capstone

The Study Box

A phone-locking box concept designed to help reduce distractions during study sessions.

During my senior year in a STEM program, my group completed a year-long capstone focused on identifying a problem and developing a product concept to address it. We named our project the Study Box, a box designed to lock away a phone during designated study periods. Our idea was informed by research into study habits, studying methods, and time management.

We researched the problem and existing products, explored possible ideas, and presented our findings to other groups to receive feedback. After selecting our concept, we created concept art, considered suitable materials, and developed a physical prototype.

01. Research

Investigated the problem, reviewed similar ideas and products, and considered possible ways to address phone distractions during studying.

02. Concept Development

Worked as a group to select the Study Box idea and develop a concept focused on designated study periods.

03. Design & Prototyping

Created concept art, reviewed design critiques, considered materials, and built a physical prototype of the idea.

04. Presentation & Feedback

Presented our research and findings to other groups, received feedback, and considered different perspectives on the proposed product.

What I Learned

This project gave me experience working through an extended design process, researching existing solutions, developing a product concept, considering materials, building a prototype, collaborating with a team, and presenting our work for critique. It helped me understand the effort involved in taking an idea from research through design and prototyping.
